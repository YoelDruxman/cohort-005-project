# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### Refunds

**Purchase**:
A learner paying for a course once. Every Refund Request and Refund belongs to a Purchase.
_Avoid_: Order, Transaction

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member who decides Refund Requests and settles Refunds. An Admin never decides their own Refund Request.
_Avoid_: The team, Staff, Reviewer

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. An Admin approves or declines it. A purchase can have at most one Refund Request, and the Admin's decision on it is final.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Admin has decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and it immediately ends access to the course for the learner and for anyone holding a seat from that purchase.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request an Admin has turned down. It has no Refund, and the learner can't raise another Refund Request for that purchase.
_Avoid_: Rejected, Denied

**Decline Note**:
The Admin's optional explanation to the learner of why their Refund Request was declined. When there isn't one, the learner sees a standard message instead.
_Avoid_: Reason (that word belongs to the learner's side of a Refund Request), Rejection reason

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund an Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed, and the Refund can still become Issued once the money goes through.
